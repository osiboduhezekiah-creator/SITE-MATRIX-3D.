◈ QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Do I need to install anything?

No. SITE MATRIX 3D runs entirely in your browser — just open the Studio and start building.

Is the demo really free?

Yes. Demo mode gives you a full sandbox with every building tool and all 3 hologram modes. A ₦2,000/month subscription lets you create and keep unlimited projects of your own. Cancel anytime.

How does the subscription work?

Subscribe with your card through the secure in-app checkout — ₦2,000/month, cancel anytime. This demo build simulates the payment — no real charge is made — and renewals are manual here; the same flow connects to Paystack or Flutterwave for automatic live billing.

What happens if my subscription expires?

Your projects are kept safely, but creating new projects and exporting lock again until you renew. Renewing extends your access for another 30 days from the day you pay.

Will I find my work when I come back?

Yes. Your projects are saved in your browser under your account, with autosave while you work. Use Export to keep backup files.
